StreamSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

StreamSearch is a movie and television discovery application. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use StreamSearch.

1. Information You Provide

StreamSearch does not require you to create an account. You can search for titles and explore streaming availability without providing your name or email address.

2. App Preferences and Watchlist

StreamSearch offers streaming-service preferences and a watchlist. These features may store information on your device to maintain your selections. The app is not intended to require an account to use these features.

3. Movie and TV Information

StreamSearch uses The Movie Database (TMDB) to display information about movies, television programs, cast members, and streaming availability. Searches and requests for content information may be processed by TMDB and related network services. StreamSearch is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

4. Advertising

StreamSearch uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, and protect advertising, subject to applicable settings, permissions, and laws.

Learn more about Google's practices at Google Privacy Policy.

5. External Streaming Services and Links

StreamSearch may provide links to third-party streaming services and websites. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. StreamSearch does not control their information practices.

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the app and its service providers may be used to provide movie and TV search results, maintain app functionality, display and measure advertising, diagnose technical problems, and improve reliability.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers supporting content discovery, advertising, and app operation. StreamSearch does not require users to submit personal profile information to access its core search features.

8. Children's Privacy

StreamSearch is a general-audience entertainment discovery app and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please contact us.

9. Security and Retention

We aim to limit the information needed to operate StreamSearch. Information handled by third-party services is subject to their applicable retention and security practices.

10. Your Choices

You can manage applicable advertising and privacy preferences through your device settings. You may also contact us with privacy questions or requests.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as StreamSearch changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact lbhdigitaldesigns@gmail.com.
